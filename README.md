## What's here

**[Yatawara-Exposure-Inequity-Gradient](https://github.com/tomregpala/Yatawara-Exposure-Inequity-Gradient)** — R

The analysis workflow behind [our paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aeaoa.2026.100492) in *Atmospheric Environment: X* on U.S. air pollution exposure inequality, 2000–2023. Pulls 24 years of EPA readings, joins them to Census income and population records, and generates per-state figures, result tables, and diagnostics for missing counties and gaps in the historical series.

**[seniorProj-distortion-pedal](https://github.com/tomregpala/seniorProj-distortion-pedal)** — KiCad

A two-layer PCB fitting a JFET noise-gated guitar distortion circuit into a standard 1590B enclosure. The first fabricated board didn't work — the repo documents the four design faults that explain why, each with its fix, and what changes in rev 2.

**AUTO-BOTS** — CUDA, not public

Two GPU-side defenses against the Decepticon model-extraction attack, which identifies a transformer's architecture by fingerprinting kernel execution. Constant-load padding drops the attack's top-1 identification rate from 95% to 9.6% but costs a 6.6× slowdown; jitter scheduling costs 33% overhead and cuts it to 61%. [Write-up](https://tomregpala.github.io/auto-bots.html).

My exploration of embedded systems engineering through various projects on the STM32-Nucleo-F446RE Microcontroller.
